FINDING 1
Severity: Major (advisory)
Affected scope: line 20, last sentence ("When the parts do not all fit the tree's limits at once, start the parts that fit in one group, and the rest in a group of their own once the first group has reported.")
False compromise: the text treats "one wake for all reports" (a join group) and "keep every slot busy" as the same choice and picks waves. A group releases only when every member has reported (reference.md 313-315; groupNotice in src/tree/text.ts 453-470), so wave 2 cannot start until the slowest member of wave 1 ends; with 8 parts under maxChildren 5, three slots sit idle for the tail of wave 1. The plugin's refusal text already points at the other mode: "end your turn and start it once one of them has reported" (src/tree/text.ts 175-177), which only works when the overflow parts are not grouped, since an ungrouped report wakes the parent at once (reference.md 313). The plugin cannot resolve this itself (a group has no partial release), so the skill is the only place the reader learns there are two modes.
Proposed resolution (Dynamics: choose the batching by the workload): replace the sentence with "When they do not all fit the limits at once, start waves, each its own group, or leave them ungrouped and start the next as each report frees a slot." (same length).
Secondary liabilities: the ungrouped mode costs one parent turn per report and the parent must track what is still to start (the standing entry, reference.md 207-217, does this for direct children on OpenCode 2.2.0+, so the cost is turns, not bookkeeping); the parent's prompt cache is touched once per report instead of once per wave.

FINDING 2
Severity: Major (advisory)
Affected scope: lines 14-15 (the split rule and the "exactly one session" rule), with line 3's repeat of the trigger.
False compromise: the maintainers' claim (CLAUDE.md 69-70) is that the skill must repeat the brief's split rule because "the root has no brief", and every session that loads the skill pays those tokens on every later request. Tested against the code: the root does carry the rule where it matters, in its role line, which shapeContext pushes on every request of a root that has courier_spawn (src/tree/context.ts 19-22; rolePart depth===null, src/tree/text.ts 75-82): the trigger, "handing a heavy task whole to one session to keep your context, but never a small one", and both split-line forms. A child carries the full rule in its brief (splitRule, src/tree/text.ts 50-66), including "never start exactly one session for the whole task" and the stage-is-a-part clause. The root depth-0 role also says "Check and integrate what each one reports yourself" (text.ts 85-86). So of lines 14-15 only two sentences are stated nowhere else: "Several whole jobs in one prompt, such as 4 tickets, are that many parts..." and the reason for the rule, "it only adds a hop, and a chain of single hops uses up the tree's depth". The rest is a second copy for the root and a third for a child, and line 7 already sends the reader to "your brief, or the ... role line" for its own depth and limits.
Proposed resolution (Extraction: keep in the cached document only what the per-session sources cannot say): collapse lines 14-15 to "- Split or do by the rule in your brief or role line. Several whole jobs in one prompt, such as 4 tickets, are that many parts: each session runs this skill for its job, so the tree below you repeats its shape once per job. Never one session for the whole task below the root: it only adds a hop, and a chain of single hops uses up the tree's depth." (shorter than the two bullets it replaces). "One session for one stage is a part" stays in the brief, where it already is.
Secondary liabilities: (a) a sub-orchestrator whose brief has been compacted away loses the stage-is-a-part wording, since its role entry says only "as your brief says" (text.ts 90-91); instruction entries survive compaction (plugin-api-notes.md 404-405) but the brief, a transcript message, may not, so this move is safe only if the sub-orchestrator role entry gains that clause (a plugin change, outside this file); (b) the skill stops reading as a self-contained procedure for a person who opens it through /skills; (c) the root's role line is a system part on old OpenCode and a baseline entry on 2.2.0+, so nothing moves there, but any future trimming of the root role line would reopen the gap.

FINDING 3
Severity: Nit
Affected scope: line 50 ("Lost track, after a compaction or a restart: one courier_tree shows every session under you...")
False compromise: the text accepts "no polling" and "knowing where my children stand" as a pair settled by one courier_tree call after a compaction, a JSON listing of up to maxTotal sessions. The plugin already resolves this for direct children: the standing entry is a session instruction kept current by the plugin, in the baseline, and it survives compaction (reference.md 207-217; plugin-api-notes.md 384-405; snapshot 1645-1657 says "so you need not look with courier_children or courier_tree"). The skill never names it, so a reader who followed line 27 and then lost its transcript spends a tool call and a large result on what is already in its system prompt.
Proposed resolution (Preliminary Action already made by the plugin; the text only needs to point at it): "Lost track, after a compaction or a restart: your standing entry lists the sessions you started and their state; one courier_tree for what is below them, or when there is no entry. One look, not a loop." (about the same length).
Secondary liabilities: the standing entry lists at most 10 children (STANDING_MAX, text.ts 103) and only direct children, not grandchildren or OpenCode subagents; it is absent on an OpenCode before 2.2.0 or where a put failed, so the courier_tree fallback must stay named; reading it needs the reader to know the entry's name, which the skill would now introduce.

FINDING 4
Severity: Nit
Affected scope: line 7, second sentence ("If courier_spawn is not among your tools, you cannot split: do the task yourself, and report upward as your brief says if a session started you.")
False compromise: the text accepts that one cached document must serve every role, and so carries a branch for the leaf. The one-document bound is real: ctx.skill.transform is an editor over a global registry with no session context (src/skill.ts 114-124, src/index.ts 159; plugin-api-notes.md 421-429, "The same id from several locations is one entry"), so per-session tailoring is not available, and I do not raise it. But the plugin already routes the leaf away from the skill: courier_spawn, courier_recall and courier_note are removed from its tool list (context.ts 20), its brief says "do the task yourself" (text.ts 52) and its role line says the same and "report with courier_send to the session that started you" (text.ts 94-95); reference.md 227 says a leaf "is sent straight to its report". The sentence therefore repeats, for every reader, what the only reader it serves already has twice in its own prompt.
Proposed resolution (Extraction): drop the sentence; line 7 becomes "Your brief, or the opencode-courier role line in your system prompt, names your depth in the session tree and its limits." (shorter).
Secondary liabilities: a leaf that loads the skill anyway (through /skills or by reading skill://courier-orchestrate/SKILL.md) gets no instruction from the skill itself and relies on its role line alone; a root in a session with courier_spawn denied by a permission rule loses the hint, though such a root is not a courier reader either.

Examined and not raised (the plugin already resolves them, or they are bounds):
- Relaying every permission request vs keeping the tree moving (lines 29, 35): "once" goes through the top session's own rules, so a rule the person saves there passes later proposals without a prompt (reference.md 409-411; permissionAsk, src/person/text.ts 77), and the asks inbox lets the person answer any child's request with no model in between (reference.md 779-805). The design-time rule "one permission rule covers the checks" (lines 21, 35) is a sound Preliminary Action. No false compromise.
- Not polling vs not losing a child that never reports (line 27): the courier_later check-in is Beforehand Cushioning the plugin recommends in courier_spawn's own description (src/tree/tools.ts 137-138), and the failed-turn, silent-end and reminder notices (reference.md 346-386) already turn the failure into a wake. No false compromise.
- Autonomy of a child vs control by the parent (lines 21, 30, 34): the brief gives the child its own rules for findings and the person's word, the parent passes on and never judges; courier_send steers without a status, courier_stop ends a branch. Balanced; nothing accepted as inevitable.
- "Never hand the checking to another session" (line 35) against "native: true for a part that only ... checks" (line 22): a trust rule the brief states too (text.ts 63-64), treated here as an invariant of the design, not a technical contradiction; the wording tension is for a consistency reviewer, not this persona.
- Depth consumed by single hops (line 15), the lesson's 300-character limit (line 44), the limits on live sessions (lines 20, 48-49): bounds the plugin enforces, not compromises.
- Prompt-cache stability vs per-session tailoring in general: a real bound of the skill registry (see Finding 4), and the plugin already puts the per-session text (role, lessons, standing) in baseline instruction entries, so the skill rightly stays one stable document.
